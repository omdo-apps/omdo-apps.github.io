The game

Tap. Fling. Orbit.

Your comet circles a planet. Tap at the right moment to launch it along its path and land in the next planet's orbit. Hop at the first chance to chain combos, and every landing plays the next note of a melody.

Orbit Hop: Space Jump

Arcade · Android · Plays offline

A one-tap arcade game about timing. The further you hop, the faster your comet circles, the smaller the planets get and the wider the gaps. Every 15 planets you enter a new galaxy zone. Runs take a minute or two, so it fits into a short break.

How to play

  1. Tap PLAY, then tap to launch your comet along its path.
  2. Land inside the next planet's orbit ring to be captured.
  3. Hop at the first chance to build a combo, up to ×5.
  4. Missed? Tap Retry and go for a longer run.

What's in the game

  • One-touch gameplayTap anywhere on the screen. Easy to learn, hard to master.
  • Combos up to ×5Hop at the first chance to multiply your score. A gold timer around your planet shows how long you have.
  • Musical hopsEvery landing plays the next note of a melody. A high combo lifts it an octave.
  • Perfect landingsFly straight at a planet's centre for bonus points and a harmony note.
  • Six galaxy zonesDeep Space, Ember Nebula, Frost Belt, Toxic Cloud, Violet Storm and Golden Core, each with its own colours.
  • Surprises on the wayUnstable planets that throw you off, treasure planets full of stars and wormholes that skip ahead.
  • Dodge asteroidsFrom the Frost Belt on, rocks drift across the gaps. Time your hop around them.
  • Power-upsA shield that saves one miss, a magnet that pulls in every star, and slow-mo to help you aim.
  • Daily GalaxyA new galaxy every day, the same for every player. Beat the daily goal for bonus stars.
  • Missions and daily giftsAlways three missions to chase, and a gift that grows every day you come back.
  • 25 comet skinsMoons, rockets, satellites, atoms and more, each with its own trail. Five special skins can only be earned.
  • Live recordsA banner the moment you pass your best score, and a line marking the furthest planet you've reached.
  • Pauses when you need itPress Back or leave the app mid-run and the game pauses. Sound and vibration have on/off switches.
  • About adsA small banner below the game, rewarded ads only when you choose one (continue a run or double your stars) and an occasional ad between runs, never mid-hop. No in-app purchases, no account.

About

Orbit Hop: Space Jump is a one-tap arcade game about timing. A comet circles a planet, and one tap flings it along its path toward the next planet's orbit. Hopping at the first chance builds combos up to ×5, and every landing plays the next note of a melody. Runs pass through six galaxy zones with unstable planets, wormholes, asteroids and power-ups, and a new Daily Galaxy each day is the same for every player. Stars from runs, missions and daily gifts unlock 25 comet skins. The game is supported by ads, with no in-app purchases.
