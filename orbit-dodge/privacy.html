Privacy Policy for Orbit Dodge

  • Effective date: 23 November 2026
  • Developer: OMDO
  • Contact: omdo.games21@gmail.com

Summary

The game itself does not collect, share or sell any personal information. The game does not connect to the internet. If you join a test or email us, we receive your email address, as described under "Testing and contacting us" below.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves two things in its private storage on your device:

  • your best survival time, and
  • whether you turned the sound effects on or off.

The game never sends them to us or to anyone else. The only exception is Android's own device backup, which is described in the next section. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Device backup

If you have Android's backup feature turned on, Android may include these two settings in your device backup, so your best time can come back on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your own backup settings. We cannot see or access it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not use the internet.
  • It shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.
  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It uses no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising ID.
  • It does not ask for any device permissions, such as location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Google Play

When you download Orbit Dodge from Google Play, Google processes information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google provides app developers with statistics and crash reports about their apps, such as install counts and device models. We use this only to fix problems and improve the game.

Testing and contacting us

If you join a test of the game, or email us, we receive the email address and anything you choose to send us. We use it only to run the test or to reply to you, and we don't share it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

The game itself does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: omdo.games21@gmail.com
