Privacy Policy for Orbit Dodge

  • Effective date: 23 November 2026
  • Developer: OMDO
  • Contact: omdo.games21@gmail.com

Summary

Orbit Dodge is a game by OMDO. The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure those ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the game collects some information from your device, such as your device's IP address, advertising ID and how you interact with ads, and sends it to Google. OMDO does not receive this information about you.

Your game progress and settings stay on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in its private storage on your device:

  • your best Endless time, your stars for each level and the skins you have unlocked and chosen;
  • simple play totals, such as stardust collected, close calls and runs played;
  • your sound and haptics settings.

The game does not send these to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Google's consent tool (described below) also stores your ad privacy choices on your device, so that the game remembers them.

Ads in the game (Google AdMob)

Orbit Dodge shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is Google AdMob's software inside the app. The ads are of two kinds:

  • rewarded ads, which only play when you choose to watch one, for example to continue a run after a crash or to unlock a skin early; and
  • full-screen ads, shown from time to time when you leave a results screen.

The game shows no banner ads.

When ads are requested and shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects the following information and shares it with Google:

  • IP address. Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device (for example, your city or region, not your exact position).
  • App interactions. Information about how you interact with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and ad video views.
  • Diagnostic information. Information about the performance of the app and the ad software, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers. Your device's Android advertising ID and app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google uses this information to show ads, to measure how ads perform (analytics) and to prevent fraud and abuse. Depending on your choices and where you live, the ads may be personalised (based on your interests) or non-personalised (based on general information, such as the app you are using and your approximate location).

This information is sent to Google in encrypted form (using TLS). Google processes it under its own privacy policy. OMDO does not receive your IP address, advertising ID or other device identifiers from AdMob; we only see summary reports, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

To learn more about how Google uses this information:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your ad privacy choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform when you first open it. The form explains how your data is used for ads and asks for your choices, including whether you agree to personalised ads. If you do not consent, the game can still show ads, but they will be non-personalised or limited ads.

You can change or withdraw your choices at any time: open Settings in the game and tap Privacy options. This reopens the consent form. The Privacy options button appears in the regions where Google's consent form applies.

The ad partners Google may work with in these regions are listed by Google at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903.

Other ways to control ads

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). After deleting it, ads are no longer linked to that ID.
  • Google ad settings: you can manage how Google personalises ads at https://myadcenter.google.com/.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the game stops all collection by the game and its ad software.

Internet and offline play

The game needs an internet connection only to load ads and Google's consent form. You can play the whole game without a connection; ads and the offers that need an ad are then not available.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in, and no in-app purchases.
  • It does not use your precise location and does not ask for location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.
  • OMDO does not add any analytics or tracking tools of its own. The only third-party software that collects data is Google's ad software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent tool).
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not receive any to sell.

Google Play

When you download Orbit Dodge from Google Play, Google processes information under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google gives app developers statistics and crash reports about their apps, such as install counts and device models. We use these only to fix problems and improve the game.

Testing and contacting us

If you join a test of the game, or email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send us. We use it only to run the test or to reply to you, and we don't share it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

Orbit Dodge is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, contact us at omdo.games21@gmail.com.

Device backup

If you have Android's backup feature turned on, Android may include the game's saved progress and settings in your device backup, so they can come back on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your own backup settings. We cannot see or access it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects what data the game collects, we will update the policy before the version with that change is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: omdo.games21@gmail.com
