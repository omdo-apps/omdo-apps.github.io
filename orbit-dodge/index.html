The game

A glowing orb circles a blazing star in deep space, and asteroids, comets and solar flares come for it. One tap reverses your orbit. Play Endless for your best time, or clear 24 levels for stars.

Orbit Dodge: Asteroid Arcade

Arcade · Android · Endless + 24 levels

A one-tap arcade game about timing. In Endless, the longer you last, the faster and more often the asteroids come. In Levels, each stage sets one goal and two bonus targets across four sectors of new hazards. Runs start with a single tap and restart just as fast, so it fits into a short break.

How to play

  1. Tap anywhere to start.
  2. Tap to switch your orbit between clockwise and counter-clockwise.
  3. Don't touch the asteroids.
  4. Crashed? Tap Play Again and go for a longer run.

What's in the game

  • One tap flips your orbitTap anywhere on the screen, with either hand.
  • Asteroids from every edgeEach one is aimed near your orbit.
  • 24 levels in 4 sectorsComets with warning lines, solar flares that burn part of your orbit, swarms and a gravity well. Earn up to 3 stars per level, 72 in all.
  • Skins to unlock8 orbs, 6 trails and 6 stars. Unlock them by playing: earn stars, clear sectors, collect stardust. They change the look only.
  • Endless: it only gets fasterAsteroids and your orb speed up until full speed at 50 seconds. Your score is the seconds you survive.
  • Fair hitsHit circles match the rocks you see, and your orb's is a little smaller than its glow.
  • Close callsSkim past a rock and the action slows for a split second. Chain them from CLOSE to GREAT, WILD and ORBITAL. In Levels, some goals ask for them.
  • Beat your best timeSaved on your phone, with a short jingle when you beat it.
  • Pauses when you need itTap the pause button, leave the app or press Back mid-run and the game waits for you.
  • Sound and hapticsOriginal sound effects and light haptic taps, each with an on/off switch in Settings.
  • HD spaceA blazing star, 3D-shaded asteroids in four materials, parallax stars and a glowing trail. Crashes shatter and burst.
  • About adsRewarded ads only when you choose one: continue a run or unlock a skin early. Occasional ads after a results screen, never mid-play. No banners, no in-app purchases, no account. The whole game plays offline.

About

Orbit Dodge: Asteroid Arcade is a one-tap arcade game about timing. A glowing orb circles a blazing star while asteroids, comets and solar flares close in, and the player's only move is to reverse the orbit. Endless mode scores the seconds survived; 24 levels in four sectors award up to three stars each and unlock orb, trail and star skins. The game is supported by ads, with no in-app purchases.
