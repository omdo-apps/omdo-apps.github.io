Orbit Hop: Space Jump

Arcade · Android · Plays offline

Your comet circles a planet. Tap at the right moment to fling it along its path and land in the next planet's orbit. Chain hops into combos, explore new galaxy zones, and see how far you can go.

  • One-touch gameplayEasy to learn, hard to master.
  • Combos up to ×5Hop at the first chance to multiply your score.
  • Musical hopsEvery landing plays the next note of a melody.
  • Six galaxy zonesFrom Deep Space to the Golden Core.
  • Daily GalaxyA new galaxy every day, the same for everyone.
  • 25 comet skinsMoons, rockets, satellites, atoms and more, each with its own trail.
  • Dodge asteroidsRocks drift across the later zones. Hop around them.
  • Power-upsA star magnet, and slow-mo to help you aim.

Orbit Dodge

Arcade · Android · Coming soon

A glowing orb circles a blazing star as asteroids drift in from every edge. You never leave your orbit: tap to reverse it and slip past the rocks. Your score is how many seconds you survive.

  • One tap flips your orbitClockwise, counter-clockwise, dodge.
  • Asteroids from every edgeEach one aimed near your orbit.
  • It only gets fasterSpeeds up until full speed at 50 seconds.
  • Beat your best timeSurvival is your score, in seconds.
  • 24 levels in 4 sectorsComets, solar flares, swarms and gravity.
  • Skins to unlockOrbs, trails and stars, earned by playing.
  • Close callsSkim a rock and time slows for a moment.
  • HD spaceA blazing star and 3D-shaded asteroids.
