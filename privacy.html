Privacy Policy — Orbit Hop

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Orbit Hop ("the app") is developed by OMDO ("we"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information ourselves. Your high score, stars and unlocked skins are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location
  • device and app information, and information about how you interact with ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button on the main menu. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
